
# CS 255 – System Analysis and Design

## DriverPass Project Reflection

**1. Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?**

The client for this project was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help people prepare for their driving tests. They needed a system where customers could register, choose training packages, schedule driving lessons, and take online practice tests. The system also needed to help employees manage appointments, keep track of student progress, and protect customer information.

**2. What did you do particularly well?**

I think I did well understanding what the client wanted and breaking everything down into different parts. I was able to identify the main requirements and use diagrams to show how the system would work. I also made sure the design included the different users and what each person needed access to.

**3. If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?**

If I could change one part of my work, I would spend more time on the UML diagrams. Some of them were challenging because I had to make sure the symbols, connections, and steps were correct. I would improve them by making the diagrams easier to follow and checking each one more carefully against the requirements.

**4. How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?**

I started by reviewing the DriverPass interview and identifying what the customer and employees needed from the system. I used those requirements to plan features like scheduling appointments, taking practice tests, managing accounts, and tracking reservations. I learned that understanding the user's needs is important because even if a system works, it won't be useful if it doesn't solve the problems the client is having.

**5. How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?**

When designing software, I like to understand the problem first before trying to build anything. I would start by gathering requirements, identifying the users, and organizing the main functions. Then I would use diagrams to see how everything connects and how the system should work. In the future, I would continue using UML diagrams, reviewing client feedback, and testing different situations before moving forward with development.
